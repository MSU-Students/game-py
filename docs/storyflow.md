## 1.Synopsis 
    The peaceful skies are under attack! Enemy planes have invaded and are trying to take control of the airspace. You are the best pilot in the fleet, and it is your mission to take off, shoot down the enemies, and protect your homeland. Can you defeat their leader and bring peace back to the skies? 

## 2.Characters 
    - Player (The Hero): You are a skilled pilot flying a powerful fighter plane. Your goal is to defend your territory and defeat the invasion.
    - Enemies: Rogue pilots and dangerous aircraft trying to take over. They come in different sizes and strengths. X`
    - The Boss: A huge, heavily armored enemy plane that appears at the end of each level or game.

## 3.Story Flow 
    Introduction 
        - Setting: High up in the sky. 
        - Normal Life: The sky is calm and peaceful. 

    The Inciting Incident
        - Suddenly, warning sirens sound! A fleet of enemy planes appears out of nowhere and starts attacking. 
        - The base calls you: "We need you! Take off immediately and stop them!" 

    Rising Action 
        - You take off and start fighting. 
        - You encounter waves of enemy planes. Some are fast, some are slow but tough. - As you progress, the enemies get stronger and more numerous. 
        - You collect power-ups and upgrades to make your plane stronger.

    Climax
        - You reach the enemy's main base or formation. 
        - The Big Boss Plane appears! It has heavy armor and strong weapons. 
        - This is the hardest battle. You must dodge attacks and find its weak points to destroy it.

    Falling Action 
        - After defeating the boss, the remaining enemies start to retreat. 
        - The sky is clear again. The invasion is stopped.

    Resolution 
        - "Mission Complete! Peace is restored to the skies." 
        - The game ends, but you can play again to beat your high score!
